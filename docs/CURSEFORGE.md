# CraftBoard

**Retail's crafting orders, brought to WoW: Forever. Without the spam.**

Anyone who has played Classic knows the routine: "LF Leatherworker for Dark Leather Belt, have mats,
tips". Ten times in Trade, every few minutes, hoping the right person happens to be reading.
Crafters miss the requests, buyers get no answers, and Trade turns into noise.

Retail solved this years ago with crafting orders. Forever keeps the Classic world, so it does not
have them. CraftBoard adds them back, built entirely from addon messages so it needs nothing from
Blizzard's servers.

## What it does

- **Your recipe book is shared automatically.** Open a profession window once. From then on, every
  CraftBoard user on your realm can see what you can craft.
- **Find who can make anything.** Search an item, see every crafter who knows it, online first, and
  whisper them with one click. Reagents show have/need from your own bags.
- **Post a request to the board.** Crafters see open requests, with your note and quantity, and
  offer with one click. No chat spam, no repeating yourself.
- **Still useful alone.** With nobody else installed it shows what you can craft right now and
  what you are missing.

## Looks like it belongs

CraftBoard is built from the same frame, fonts and textures as Forever's new Professions window.
Category headers, the recipe page, the Create row and the side tabs all match, so it feels like a
tab Blizzard forgot to add rather than a third-party window.

## Fair and safe

- **No gold in the protocol.** Tips are agreed in whispers, like they always were. Nothing here
  resembles an auction house or GDKP.
- **Non-combat.** Unaffected by Forever's combat addon restrictions.
- **Bind-on-Pickup items are never listed.** You will not be asked for something you cannot hand over.
- **Guild first, realm second.** Works with two guildmates; the hidden realm channel lights up as
  more people install it.

## Getting started

1. Install, log in, and open each of your profession windows once.
2. Open CraftBoard from the minimap button, the button in the Professions window, or `/cb`.
3. Search for an item, or post a request.

Tell your guild. The board is only as good as the people on it.

**Beta.** The sync layer is new; if anything looks odd, `/cb debug` and a BugSack report help.
