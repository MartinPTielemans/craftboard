# CraftBoard

**A crafting companion for WoW: Forever: level your professions, plan and craft, and find
crafters or customers without the Trade spam.**

Forever brings back the Classic world, and with it the Classic crafting grind: working out what to
craft next, hunting reagents across your alts, and repeating "LF Leatherworker, have mats" in Trade
hoping the right person reads it. CraftBoard lives in Forever's own Professions window and takes
care of all of it.

## Level smarter

- **Plan tab.** Your recipes by skill-up color, the best next craft from what is in your bags, how
  many crafts reach the next rank, and when to visit your trainer.
- **Craft and queue.** Queue what you want to make, then Craft next works through it with the
  profession window open. At a vendor, one button buys the missing thread, vials and flux.
- **Your alts count.** Reagents on your other characters show up in reagent slots and tooltips.
  Recipe tooltips say which of your characters knows a recipe or can learn it.
- **Cooldowns and ranks.** Transmutes and Mooncloth are tracked on every character, with a quiet
  line when one is ready or when you can train the next rank.

## Find crafters, help others

- **Your recipe book is shared automatically** with guildmates and realm players who run
  CraftBoard. Search any item and see who can make it, online first.
- **Requests.** Post a request with a quantity and a note; crafters see it and offer with a click.
  Requests you can craft are listed first, with what you are missing.
- **Seen in chat.** "LF enchanter" and "WTB [item]" lines from Trade are listed too, so it works
  even when nobody else has the addon yet.
- **Trade window help.** One click casts a requested enchant on your partner's item.

## Looks like it belongs

Built from the same frame, fonts and textures as Forever's Professions window, in English, German,
French and Spanish.

## Fair and safe

- **Nothing happens on its own.** No whisper, invite, trade or chat line is ever sent without your
  click.
- **No gold on the board.** Tips are agreed in whispers, like always.
- **Private.** Chat lines stay in memory for 30 minutes; your crafting history stays on your
  computer.
- **Non-combat.** Unaffected by Forever's combat addon restrictions.

## Getting started

1. Install, log in, and open each of your profession windows once.
2. Open CraftBoard from its tab in the Professions window, the minimap button, or `/cb`.
3. Check the Plan tab, search Find, or post a request.
